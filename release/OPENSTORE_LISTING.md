## Full Description (Copy-Paste into OpenStore)

Settle into the driver’s seat and cruise toward a glowing horizon in **Horizon Roads 3D** — a relaxing, procedurally generated 3D road-trip game inspired by *slowroads.io*, built natively for Ubuntu Touch 24.04 (`OpenGL ES 2.0` + `Qt5` + `Lomiri.Components` + `QtSensors`).

### Features
- **6 Seamless Procedural Biomes & Regions:**
  - **Oregon · Pacific Redwoods** — misty coastal cliffs & towering evergreens
  - **Iowa · Heartland Farms** — rolling golden wheat hills, classic red barns, silos & spinning windmills
  - **Arizona · Sonoran Red Rocks** — terraced terracotta mesas & saguaro cacti
  - **Vermont · Maple Suburbs** — cozy two-story homes with warm glowing evening windows & autumn maples
  - **Metro · Downtown Skyline** — illuminated glass skyscrapers & overhead highway streetlights
  - **Colorado · Alpine Pass** — snow-dusted peaks & frosted spruces
- **Dynamic 24-Hour Sky & 4 Weather Modes:**
  - Continuous `Dawn` → `Noon` → `Golden Sunset` → `Starry Midnight` sky shader (or lock your favorite hour!)
  - `Clear`, `Rainstorm` (with wet asphalt reflections & animated windshield wipers!), `Misty Fog`, and `Snowfall`
- **3 Distinct Cars with Bespoke Cockpits & 3rd-Person View:**
  - **`'68 Canyon Cruiser` (Classic):** Walnut woodgrain dashboard, brushed chrome analog needles, mahogany wheel & warm V8 rumble
  - **`'94 Nomad Wagon` (Comfy):** Soft touring interior, emerald-lit gauges, dashboard coffee mug, rooftop luggage box & smooth cruiser ride
  - **`Apex GT-RS` (Fun):** Matte carbon cockpit, flat-bottom sport wheel, digital RPM bar graph & crisp flat-6 sing
- **Zen Auto-Drive + Phone Tilt Steering:**
  - **Zen Cruise Mode:** Let the car steer itself effortlessly down the winding highway while you drag across the windshield to look out the side windows and listen to the radio
  - **Tilt Wheel Mode:** Steer naturally by tilting your phone (`QtSensors` accelerometer with one-tap wheel calibration), or use touch steering
  - **3 Procedural FM Radio Stations:** *92.5 Sunset Chill*, *101.3 Lo-Fi Keys*, and *107.9 Horizon Pad*, blended with speed-reactive engine purr, road whoosh & rain patter
